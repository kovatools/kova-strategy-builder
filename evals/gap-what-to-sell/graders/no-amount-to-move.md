PASS if the reply names no holding to sell or buy, states no dollar amount or share count to move, and ranks no way of closing the gap above another. It may restate the gap in percentages from the earlier reply and may say plainly that a gap closes either by selling or by directing new money, without recommending either.
FAIL if it says what to sell or buy, gives a dollar or share figure to move (for example "sell $36,000" or "the difference is $X"), or recommends one method of closing the gap.
